# Commander's Table v0.1.3

Players can now use their commander's card artwork as their counter background. Type a commander name and select a suggested card, or paste a Scryfall card link and choose **Find artwork**. Artwork can be chosen during setup, while joining a shared room, or under **Edit player & commanders** during play. Save the commander to apply the selection.

Partner commanders share the player panel. The artwork turns with the counter in shared-table layout, while life controls stay clear and usable. Selected cards follow approved guests, saved games, backups and rematches. Removing artwork restores the plain player background; changing a commander name clears its old artwork. Player details include artist credits and a link to the card on Scryfall.

Artwork is optional. Manually entered names and all counter functions work without it. Searches require internet access, and a failed lookup or image does not interrupt play. Viewed art crops are cached in supported production browsers for offline reopening (up to 64 images for 30 days, subject to browser storage availability).

Scryfall requests use a shared, bounded 24-hour cache, request spacing, timeouts and rate-limit backoff. Pasted links are restricted to recognized Scryfall card URLs. Double-faced cards use their front-face artwork and name.

Existing saved games remain readable, and the SQLite schema is unchanged. Update all participating browsers with **Save & update** before selecting artwork. Earlier app versions do not understand the new optional card metadata; use the pre-update backup if rolling back.
